Last updated: 2026-09-19

1. About New Kingdom

New Kingdom ("NK") is a personal, local desktop application run by its owner on her own computer. It helps the owner prepare, organise and manage social media content. NK is not a public service, is not offered to the general public, has no public user accounts and does not sell any product or service.

2. Who may use it

NK is used only by its owner, with the owner's own social media accounts. These terms exist to describe how NK connects to external platforms such as TikTok, Instagram, YouTube or X.

3. Connecting external accounts

NK can connect to an external platform only through that platform's official authorisation flow (OAuth). The owner signs in on the platform's own page; NK never sees or stores the account password. The owner can disconnect an account in NK at any time, which removes the local authorisation.

4. Publishing

NK may prepare drafts of posts locally. Publishing to an external platform through its API happens only after the owner's explicit approval in NK. Content is published to the owner's own accounts only.

5. Third-party platforms

External platforms have their own terms, policies and API rules (for example the TikTok, Meta/Instagram, Google/YouTube and X terms). The owner is responsible for following them. NK does not change or override those terms, and platforms may limit or withdraw API access at any time.

6. No warranty

NK is a private tool provided as is, without warranties. Features may change or stop working at any time, including because an external platform changes its API.

7. Changes

These terms may be updated when NK changes. The date at the top shows the latest revision.
